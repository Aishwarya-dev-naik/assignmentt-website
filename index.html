General Term of Binomial Expansion

1. Definition

A binomial is an algebraic expression consisting of two terms connected by a plus or minus sign. Examples of binomials are (a+b), (x-y), and (2x+3y).

When a binomial is raised to a positive integer power, its expansion produces a sequence of terms. The Binomial Theorem provides a systematic method for finding this expansion without multiplying the binomial repeatedly.

In a binomial expansion, the coefficients are determined by the binomial coefficients. The general term gives a convenient way of finding any required term directly, without writing the complete expansion.

(a+b)ⁿ = C(n,0)aⁿ + C(n,1)aⁿ⁻¹b + C(n,2)aⁿ⁻²b² + ... + C(n,n)bⁿ

The powers of the first variable decrease from n to 0, while the powers of the second variable increase from 0 to n. At the same time, the expansion contains exactly n+1 terms.

Therefore, instead of expanding the entire expression, we can use the general-term formula to directly obtain a particular term or its coefficient.

2. General Term

For the binomial expansion of (a+b)ⁿ, the general term is represented by Tr+1. It gives the term in the expansion corresponding to a particular value of r.

Tr+1 = C(n,r)an-rbr

where:

  • n is the power of the binomial.
  • r takes the values 0, 1, 2, ..., n.
  • C(n,r) is the binomial coefficient.
  • Tr+1 represents the (r+1)th term.
C(n,r) = n! / [r!(n-r)!]

If the required term is the kth term, then r = k−1. Hence,

Tk = C(n,k−1)an-k+1bk−1

This formula is particularly useful when we need to find a particular term, a coefficient, a term independent of a variable, or the value of r corresponding to a required power.

3. Examples

Example 1

Find the coefficient of x⁵y² in (x+y)⁷.

General term: Tr+1 = C(7,r)x7-ryr

Comparing with x⁵y², we get r = 2.

Coefficient = C(7,2) = 21

Answer: 21

Example 2

Find the coefficient of a⁵b² in (2a−3b)⁷.

General term: Tr+1 = C(7,r)(2a)7-r(−3b)r

For a⁵b², r = 2.

Coefficient = C(7,2)(2)⁵(−3)²

= 21 × 32 × 9 = 6048

Answer: 6048

Example 3

Find the coefficient of x¹² in x³(1−2x)¹⁰.

Since x³ is already present, we need x⁹ from (1−2x)¹⁰.

Therefore r = 9.

Coefficient = C(10,9)(−2)⁹

= 10 × (−512) = −5120

Answer: −5120

Example 4

Find the coefficient of x²y³ in (2x−5y)¹⁰.

Every term in the expansion of (2x−5y)¹⁰ has total degree 10.

But x²y³ has total degree 5.

Therefore, x²y³ cannot occur in the expansion.

Answer: 0

Example 5

Find the term independent of x in (x²+2/x)⁹.

General term: Tr+1 = C(9,r)(x²)9-r(2/x)r

Power of x = 2(9−r)−r = 18−3r.

For a term independent of x: 18−3r = 0

Therefore r = 6, so the required term is the 7th term.

T₇ = C(9,6) × 2⁶ = 84 × 64 = 5376

Answer: 5376
